Automatic SSL renewal setup: docs/ssl-renewal.md

After the one-time setup, a systemd timer checks renewal twice daily, publishes
the certificate into ./certs, and gracefully reloads the running Nginx container.

Manual verification from /home/ec2-user on EC2:
  sudo env EVSITE_COMPOSE_FILE=docker-compose.yml EVSITE_CERT_NAME=gupevolution.click-0004 bash scripts/renew-certificates.sh --dry-run

Use the actual production Compose filename if different.
